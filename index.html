Child Safety Standards

Applies to: FeetGet – Buy & Sell Last updated: 16 July 2026 Contact: bomicke.thomasernst@passiveinvestmentslearning.online

Passive Investments Learning Ltd. is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation ("CSAE") and child sexual abuse material ("CSAM") on FeetGet – Buy & Sell.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

We have zero tolerance for content or conduct that sexually exploits, abuses, endangers, grooms, solicits, or otherwise harms children. The following are strictly prohibited:

  • Uploading, sharing, requesting, promoting, or possessing CSAM.
  • Sexualization, grooming, coercion, trafficking, sextortion, or exploitation of minors.
  • Attempts to arrange sexual contact or inappropriate private communication with a minor.
  • Content that identifies, targets, threatens, or endangers a child.
  • Any activity that violates applicable child-safety or child-protection laws.

2. In-App Reporting

Users can report child-safety concerns, accounts, listings, messages, images, or other content directly within the app. Reports are reviewed and handled as promptly as reasonably possible, with urgent child-safety matters prioritized.

Users may also report concerns by email to bomicke.thomasernst@passiveinvestmentslearning.online. Please include relevant details that can help us identify the account or content. Do not send or forward suspected CSAM by email.

3. Enforcement

Where we identify a violation, we may remove content, restrict features, suspend or permanently terminate accounts, preserve relevant records where legally permitted, and take other measures necessary to protect users and the public.

4. Reports to Authorities

We comply with applicable child-safety laws and reporting obligations. Where required, we report suspected CSAM or child sexual exploitation to the relevant regional or national authorities and cooperate with lawful investigations.

5. Prevention Practices

  • Accessible reporting tools for users.
  • Review and moderation of reported content and accounts.
  • Removal and account action when prohibited content or conduct is identified.
  • Preservation and disclosure of information when required by law.
  • Periodic review of our child-safety practices and standards.

6. Designated Child-Safety Contact

Questions regarding our CSAE and CSAM prevention practices, policy compliance, or child-safety reporting may be directed to:

Passive Investments Learning Ltd.
Email: bomicke.thomasernst@passiveinvestmentslearning.online

7. Emergency Situations

If a child is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service or law-enforcement authority. The in-app reporting system and company email are not emergency services.
